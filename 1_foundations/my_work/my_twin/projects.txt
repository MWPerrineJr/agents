Projects completed:
This repo houses completed projects. The goal is to showcase projects that exhibit proficiencies in data science. 

The projects included in this repo are :

1. Churn Analysis: 
https://github.com/MWPerrineJr/completed-projects/tree/main/Churn%20Analysis
Customer turnover is a concern for business across many 
industries. The ability to identify at risk clients before 
they leave is essential to maintain marketshare. 
Understanding customer behavior and migration habits 
the firm is able to craft programs to promote client 
retention. This analysis uses a sophisticated artificial
 neural network to accomplish this goal.

2. Fraud Detection:
https://github.com/MWPerrineJr/completed-projects/tree/main/Fraud%20Detection
In this project I used synthetic data to analyze transaction 
data to identify fraudulent transactions. The data for this 
analysis was obtained from Kaggle. To accomplish this goal,
 I completed a series of transformations. To complete the 
 analysis, I built a pipeline and ran a logistic regression. 
 In addition, I used the data to build an app to identify 
 a fraudulent transaction.

3 X-Ray Analysis:
https://github.com/MWPerrineJr/completed-projects/tree/main/XRay%20Analysis
Innovation drives medical advancements. We're seeing more
technical advancements happening in the medical field.
These advancements are responsible for improvements in
quality of life for millions of individuals. One of 
these advancements is the use of machine learning and
AI in medical diagnosis. In this analysis I built a
convolutional neural network (CNN) to identify the
 differences between heathy and diseased lungs.

Businesses are always looking to gain a competitive advantage.

2. Heart Attack Prediction:
Health care is a personal topic. Everyone wants to live
a happy and healthy life. Sometimes we need help to achieve
that goal. The heart attack prediction app is geared to
shed light on the contributing factors. The analysis will
use various factors to predict individuals at high risk
for a heart attack and hopefully reduce their risks.

Everyday people are rolling the dice and losing money. 
With this tool investors can find the right currency to
invest. The tool will pull crypto data and build additional
features for analysis. In addition the tool will include
a professional back test. In addition a sophisticated
machine learning algorithm will give the investor 
confidence when making investment decisions.
