# Banoffee Pie Recipe Summary

Source: Allrecipes — *Banoffee Pie*

## Why this recipe stands out
- Classic British banoffee pie with a buttery graham cracker crust
- Rich dulce de leche layer
- Fresh banana slices and homemade whipped cream
- Easy enough for home bakers, but impressive for guests

## Ingredients
- Graham cracker crumbs
- Melted butter
- White sugar
- Dulce de leche
- Bananas, sliced
- Heavy cream
- Powdered sugar
- Optional garnish: grated chocolate or toffee bits

## Method
1. Preheat the oven and make a crust by mixing graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sugar.
2. Press the crust into a pie plate and bake until lightly golden. Let it cool completely.
3. Spread dulce de leche over the cooled crust.
4. Arrange banana slices evenly over the caramel layer.
5. Whip the heavy cream with powdered sugar until stiff peaks form.
6. Spread the whipped cream over the bananas.
7. Chill before serving, and garnish with grated chocolate if desired.

## Tips from reviewers
- Use 3 bananas for a fuller pie.
- Some reviewers prefer putting the bananas under the toffee to reduce mess.
- Canned dulce de leche is a convenient shortcut.
- Keep it chilled and serve within 24 hours for best texture.
- A light dusting of grated chocolate or coffee-flavored whipped cream is a popular upgrade.

## Short takeaway
This banoffee pie is a fast, crowd-pleasing dessert with buttery crust, silky caramel, fresh bananas, and fluffy cream. It’s best served well chilled and finished with a little chocolate on top.
