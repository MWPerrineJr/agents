# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-car Sales Fleet

## Quick comparison

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | About $43,630 | About $37,795 |
| EPA range | About 294–321 miles | About 260–320 miles |
| Fast charging | Up to 250 kW; strong Supercharger advantage | Up to 150 kW; Tesla Supercharger access in supported markets |
| Passenger capacity | 5 standard, available 7-seat option | 5 seats |
| Cargo space | About 66 cu ft | About 59.7 cu ft |
| Fleet fit | Strong range, charging, and utility | Lower entry price, good range, slightly less utility |

## Fleet assessment

- **Tesla Model Y** is the stronger operational choice if the fleet prioritizes range, charging convenience, and cargo utility.
- **Ford Mustang Mach-E** is attractive on purchase price and remains a practical EV for sales reps with predictable routes and regular charging access.
- For a 100-car fleet, the Tesla’s Supercharger access and higher efficiency can reduce driver downtime and simplify route planning.
- The Ford is the better value if upfront cost matters most and the team is comfortable with slightly lower range and charging speed.

## Recommendation

**Choose the Tesla Model Y** for the 100-car sales fleet. Its longer range, faster charging, and stronger cargo utility make it the better fit for high-utilization sales driving, even though it costs more upfront. The Mach-E is the budget-friendlier alternative, but the Model Y is the safer fleet standard for minimizing charging friction and maximizing daily usability.
